The infrastructure for the study is in place and verified: a seeded, config-driven pipeline with recorded provenance, a faithful and tested port of FLAP and two baselines, a reproducible 19{,}242-question pool, and a scoring pipeline whose every result is cross-checked against lm-eval. The first measurements answer RQ1: FLAP produces a steep accuracy--cost ladder on a 1.7\,B model (perplexity $\times$1.40 at 20\%, $\times$6.74 at 50\%) and keeps its advantage over Wanda-SP and Magnitude-SP. They also show that on a T4 at batch size 1 width pruning reduces prefill latency, energy and memory but not decode latency, so the cost model used for routing must be measured per device rather than assumed proportional to parameter count. Whether routing pays off (RQ2) depends on the oracle headroom, which the outcome runs now in progress will measure; the analysis separates genuine complementarity between variants from lucky guesses by comparing with independence and chance references.
